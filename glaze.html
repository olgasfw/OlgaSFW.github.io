The design opportunity: help ceramic makers move between visual inspiration and the practical information they need to choose a glaze.

A calm catalogue structure creates room for both material character and technical detail.

The proposed experience.

DESIGN INTENT
01 / DISCOVER

Browse by what matters.

A visual library with clear labels and useful filtering would help makers narrow their options.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Put context beside color.

A detail view would place finish, firing information, and references together.

03 / COLLECT

Build a personal studio.

Saved glazes and test notes would connect discovery with the next making session.
